Experiences

Three ways to travel

Small group safaris

Departures with 4–8 guests. Shared 4×4, shared camp nights, a lower price without losing the wild. Good for solo travellers and friends who want company on the plains.

Family safaris

Built for children and grandparents: shorter game drives, family rooms or connecting tents, picnic lunches and guides who like questions.

Private safaris

Your vehicle, your pace, your lodges. Honeymoons, slow travel and custom routes — we move when you are ready, and we stop when the light is good.
